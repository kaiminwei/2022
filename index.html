Kaimin Wei/魏凯敏


Kaimin Wei is a Full Professor in the College of Information Science and Technology at Jinan University (JNU). He obtained his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Beihang University (also called Beijing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics). In 2013, he participated in a joint doctoral program at the University of Aizu in Japan. From 2024 to 2025, he served as a Visiting Professor at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in Singapore. He was selected as a Young Pearl River Scholar.

His research interests are mainly in Edge Intelligence/Crowdsensing (algorithm optimization/reinforcement learning), Artificial Intelligence Security (privacy protection/inversion attacks/adversarial machine learning/federated learning), Intelligent Recommendation.
